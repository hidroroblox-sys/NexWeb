# Escape Tsunami For Brainrots: brainrot catalog, economy and rebirth (for an egg-based remake)

> **How these notes were made (read first).** Every direct page fetch failed in this research session. WebFetch returned DNS errors (ENOTFOUND) for all hosts, and the egress proxy refused curl to fandom.com, beebom.com, techwiser.com, sportskeeda.com and web.archive.org. **Every number below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries and snippets of the cited pages, not from reading the pages in full.** The figures are quoted as the snippets gave them, but full tables could not be checked line by line. Treat any single-source number as "verify in-game / on the page". Guide dates seen in results range from January 2026 to September 2026. The game updates often, and many values were rebalanced over time; Celestial income, for example, appears about 5x higher in newer guides.
>
> **Game identity:** "[🌊] Escape Tsunami For Brainrots!" (Roblox place ID 131623223084840). Made by the group "Wave of Brainrots" and created 15 Dec 2025. It had about 5.6–5.7B visits by spring 2026 and a peak of about 4.99M concurrent players on 24 Jan 2026 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/131623223084840); [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots). >
> **Core loop (for context):** brainrots spawn along a long runway from the base. Rarer ones spawn farther out. The player runs out, picks brainrots up (carry limit), and runs back before a tsunami wave hits, hiding in trenches. A wave hit sends you back to the start. Brainrots placed in base slots generate $/s. Money buys Speed, Carry, Jump, base slots and brainrot levels. Rebirth resets Speed for a permanent money multiplier — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/all-brainrots-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/); [games.gg zones](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-zones-guide/); [brainrotgames.games](https://brainrotgames.games/guides/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-guide/).

## 1. Rarity tiers (order and colors)

### Takeaway
The current game (mid/late 2026) has **13 rarity tiers**. In order: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical, Cosmic, Secret, Celestial, Divine, Infinity, Paradox, Eternal. Older guides show 9 tiers (up to Celestial) or 11 (up to Infinity), because tiers were added in updates. I could not find a source that maps each tier to its official UI color.

### Cited Findings
- games.gg ranks the tiers lowest to highest as Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical, Cosmic, Secret, Celestial, Divine, Infinity, Paradox, Eternal, and says there are "over 180 brainrots across 13 rarity tiers" — [games.gg list](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-brainrots-list/); [games.gg values](https://games.gg/roblox/guides/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-brainrots-rarity-and-values/)
- Eldorado and Game8-style lists also give the same 13 tiers — [Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/all-brainrots-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/); [Game8 list](https://game8.co/games/Roblox/archives/581250)
- Bloxguides says there are 12 rarities ending at Paradox (no Eternal), and that "Paradox was added in the Carnival Update and is the rarest tier" — [bloxguidesgg](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots)
- Driffle's September 2026 list has 11 rarities ending at Infinity — [Driffle](https://driffle.com/blog/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-list-of-brainrots/)
- Earlier guides listed 9 rarities "Common through Celestial", with Celestial as the top tier at the time. Divine was later "added as a new rarity" — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/all-brainrots-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/); [Gamertweak](https://gamertweak.com/all-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/)
- The dedicated Fandom wiki's Rarities page (older snapshot) describes Infinity as the top tier, "obtainable only from the Infinity Lucky Block" — [Fandom Rarities](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Rarities)
- **Order conflict:** some sources disagree on whether Secret sits above or below Mythical/Cosmic. Most place Secret after Cosmic — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-trade-values-every-brainrot-ranked-by-income-and-rarity/)
- **Colors:** the only color found is that the Common Lucky Block is white and spawns in the Common Area — [TechWiser lucky blocks](https://techwiser.com/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-lucky-blocks-guide/). Blue and red are used for **wave types**, not rarities: blue waves are slower, red waves are fast — [Beebom walkthrough](https://beebom.com/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-walkthrough-beginners-guide/amp/)

### Inferences
- For the remake, use the 13-tier order above. Tiers 1–9 (Common to Celestial) are the "runway/zone" tiers. Tiers 10–13 (Divine, Infinity, Paradox, Eternal) are event/lucky-block/merchant tiers that never spawn as normal runway drops (see section 2 sources).
- Rarity is the main driver of base income. Each tier is roughly 3–10x the previous one at the low end (for example Common max $15 → Uncommon min $20 → Rare $100 → Epic $290 → Legendary $1.5K → Mythical $6K → Cosmic $22K → Secret $200K → Celestial $1.5M–7.5M → Divine $30M → Infinity $250M–375M → Paradox/Eternal ~$1.5–1.9B).

### Gaps
- No source found giving official rarity colors (hex or names). This needs an in-game check, for example of index/label colors in screenshots.
- The exact number of brainrots per tier in the latest version is unconfirmed. "180+" is from games.gg only.

## 3. Base / plot: slots, floors, collecting, offline earnings

### Takeaway
The base holds brainrots in slots. Extra slots are bought with cash at a signboard outside the base, one slot per purchase, at steeply rising prices (slot 11 = $1M up to about $1T). Every 10 slots adds a new floor. The cap was 31 slots in early guides and was raised to base level 40 by April 2026. Placed brainrots generate money every second, including while offline, and you collect it by standing in front of them.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Speed upgrades

### Takeaway
Speed is bought with cash in the Upgrade Shop in **+1 / +5 / +10** level packs. Price compounds at **×1.17 per level** according to the Fandom wiki, and a +5 or +10 pack costs price×1.17^5 or price×1.17^10, which makes bulk buying cheaper. Speed is the main rebirth requirement and is the stat that resets on rebirth. The max speed level is listed as **15,670** (April 2026).

### Cited Findings
- "Speed, Jumps, and Carry can be upgraded in the Upgrade Shop. These upgrades cost money" — [Fandom Survival Guide](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Survival_Guide)
- "Upgrades can be purchased in increments of 1 level, 5 levels, or 10 levels." Formula: Next Level Price = Price × 1.17; Next 5 Levels Price = Price × (1.17^5); Next 10 Levels Price = Price × (1.17^10) — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots); [Fandom Upgrades](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrades)
- Max speed level 15,670 (as of April 2026) — [allthings.how max caps](https://allthings.how/every-max-level-cap-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-explained/)
- "Money for a +1 upgrade can exceed 100 billion near speed 150", and the requirement steps up after each ten-level block — [Beebom speed](https://beebom.com/how-to-increase-speed-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/)
- "Price jumps noticeably every 10 levels" — [Noleep speed](https://noleep.com/en/escape-tsunami-brainrots-speed-guide/)
- Third-party calculator estimate to reach speed 50: about $721.77K with +1 purchases, $535.25K with +5, $355.49K with +10 (an estimate, not official) — [bloxcalculator](https://bloxcalculator.org/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-speed-upgrade-calculator)

- Robux: speed boost gamepass 129 Robux (NamuWiki). Reseller listings mention "Permanent Speed Boost" and "HUGE Permanent Speed Boost" gamepasses — [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/Escape%20Tsunami%20For%20Brainrots!); [ggwtb](https://ggwtb.com/etfb-brainrots)

### Inferences
- If price(L) = P0 × 1.17^L, the +5 and +10 packs cost only 1.17^5 ≈ 2.19x and 1.17^10 ≈ 4.81x the current single-level price, instead of the summed 6.0x and 23.3x. That gives bulk buys a big discount, which matches the calculator outcome. P0 (the level-1 price) is unknown.
- Pure ×1.17 compounding from speed ~16 to ~150 multiplies the price by about 1.17^134 ≈ 1.4e9. That is consistent with "+1 exceeds $100B near speed 150" if P0 is around $10–100. **This is speculation; verify P0 in-game.**
- The "jump every 10 levels" reports suggest there may be an extra step multiplier per 10 levels on top of ×1.17, or simply that players notice the compounding.

### Gaps
- Starting speed value and level-1 price (P0), and whether the multiplier changes after some level. No verified per-level price table was found.
- Walk speed in studs per speed level (movement formula).

## 6. Rebirth

### Takeaway
Rebirth needs a **Speed level** for Rebirths 1–22 (40, then +10 per rebirth) and gives **+0.5x money multiplier per rebirth up to 11x at Rebirth 20**. From about Rebirth 23 it also needs **base $/s** and, later, **Divine brainrots placed in the base**; from Rebirth 29 it needs **Jump + $/s + Infinity brainrots**. Rebirth resets Speed (and probably Jump); brainrots, cash and base are kept. The reported max is 20, 25, 26, 27 or 29 depending on guide date. The newest wiki says **29 = 13.25x**.

### Cited Findings
- Rebirths 1–20 (consistent across Beebom, the calculator site, TechWiser and allthings.how), as Speed required → money multiplier: R1 40 → 1.5x; R2 50 → 2x; R3 60 → 2.5x; R4 70 → 3x; R5 80 → 3.5x; R6 90 → 4x; R7 100 → 4.5x; R8 110 → 5x; R9 120 → 5.5x; R10 130 → 6x; R11 140 → 6.5x; R12 150 → 7x; R13 160 → 7.5x; R14 170 → 8x; R15 180 → 8.5x; R16 190 → 9x; R17 200 → 9.5x; R18 210 → 10x; R19 220 → 10.5x; R20 230 → 11x — [Beebom rebirth](https://beebom.com/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-rebirth-guide/); [escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.com](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.com/rebirth); [allthings.how rebirth](https://allthings.how/how-rebirth-works-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-all-20-levels/); [TechWiser rebirth](https://techwiser.com/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-rebirth/)
- Conflict: one wiki lists Rebirth 1 at 30 Speed instead of 40 — [Fandom Rebirth (alt wiki)](https://escapetsunamiforbrainnrot.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth)
- Later rebirths (Gamerant): R21 230 Speed; R22 240 Speed; R23 300 Speed + base generation $4.99 Qn/s; R24 350 Speed + $100 Qn/s + 1 Divine in base; R25 (max at the time) 360 Speed + $10 Sx/s + 3 Divine in base → 12x — [Gamerant rebirth](https://gamerant.com/roblox-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-how-rebirth-fast-all-levels/)

- The multipliers fit a simple pattern: **+0.5x per rebirth for R1–R20 (1.5x→11x), then +0.25x per rebirth for R21–R29 (11.25x→13.25x)**. This matches "26 = 12.50x" and "29 = 13.25x" exactly. - No source mentions rebirth giving extra slots or items. The reward appears to be only the money multiplier, plus unlocking higher brainrot level caps at R26/R29.
- Speed resets, but cash is kept. So the rebirth "cost" is re-buying speed with a higher income multiplier. Rebirth 1 at speed 40 is very cheap (well under $1M by the calculator estimate for speed 50), which is why guides say to rebirth early.

### Gaps
- Exact requirements for R26–R28 and the full R29 requirement numbers (Jump level, $/s amount, number of Infinity brainrots).
