## Q1. Core loop step by step (spawn → run → grab → return → place → earn → upgrade)

### Takeaway
You spawn in a safe zone where every player has a base. You run out along a linear track of rarity-named areas, grab a brainrot (1 at a time at first), and run back before a tsunami catches you. You place it on a base slot, where it earns $/second, and collect the cash by stepping on buttons. That money buys Speed (the main progression stat, which also gates rebirths), Carry capacity and Base slots. Rebirth resets Speed in exchange for a permanent money multiplier.

### Cited Findings
- Official framing (unofficial wiki summary): a simulation/tycoon by sc7ry where "you collect valuable Brainrots and deliver them to the safe zone as you avoid the waves". It covers brainrot collection/leveling, rebirth for income multipliers, and speed and carry-capacity upgrades. — [BloxInformer wiki](https://bloxinformer.com/wikis/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/)
- Developer: "created by sc7ry in collaboration with Do Big Studios™ under the community Wave of Brainrots". — [Roblox Fandom wiki page](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots)
- New players start with a carry capacity of 1: "pick up a single brainrot and immediately run back to your base". Then "Walk onto your base platform and drop the brainrot. It starts producing coins per second right away." — [allthings.how money guide](https://allthings.how/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-money-guide-fastest-ways-to-scale-your-income/) / [Beebom money guide](https://beebom.com/how-to-make-money-in-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots/) (snippet attribution)

### Inferences
- The loop to recreate is: a safe-zone hub with bases, then a run outward along a track, a pickup, a return before the wave, placing the item on a base slot to earn passive $/s, and walking over a collect pad. Money goes into Speed, Carry and Base slots. Once Speed reaches the next threshold, the player can rebirth: Speed resets to 0 and the money multiplier rises.
- Speed doubles as the main "level". It decides which areas you can reach and come back from, and it gates rebirth. In an egg version, Speed should be the core purchasable stat.

### Gaps
- No source says whether income accumulates per slot (a pad per slot, as in Steal a Brainrot) or in one shared pool. "Green buttons" (plural) suggests a pad per slot, but this is unconfirmed.
- No exact UI or controls (ProximityPrompt text or key) were found.
- No source gives the speed price formula. One calculator site reports ×1.17 per level; another guide says ×1.5 every 10 levels. Neither is confirmed. — [BloxCalculator](https://bloxcalculator.org/escape-tsunami-for-brainrots-speed-upgrade-calculator); [escapetsunamiforbrainrotscalculator.com](https://escapetsunamiforbrainrotscalculator.com/upgrades) (snippet attribution)

## Q2. Map layout (bases vs. path, corridor shape, size, zones/biomes, safe zone, walls)

### Takeaway
The map is a long linear track running outward from a safe zone where the player bases sit side by side. The track is divided into consecutive areas named after rarities: Common nearest the base, Celestial farthest, then the Tsunami Tower at the very end. Gaps or trenches between areas act as wave shelters. A wall runs along the side, with VIP-only alcoves (right side originally, both sides after an update). The Jan 2026 "Wave Wednesday" update roughly doubled the map size. I found no stud dimensions.

### Inferences
- The layout to recreate: the hub/safe zone with all player bases in a row at one end, then one long straight runway pointing away from the bases. The runway is a chain of rectangular "area" platforms in rarity order, separated by ground-level gaps or trenches that you can drop into. Wall(s) run along the sides with recessed alcoves (VIP-gated). The tower or end-goal sits at the far end, outside the waves' range. The "Untitled" first area with no spawns is probably the stretch right outside the bases.
- Because Cosmic has 2 areas and Secret has 3, the deep end of the track is longer and has more gaps. That roughly means about 12 area platforms in total (Untitled + Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical + 2 Cosmic + 3 Secret + Celestial). This is a count derived from the wiki's area list, not a confirmed platform count.
- Each area is likely tinted to match its rarity color, but no source confirmed colors or biomes.

### Gaps
- No source gives track length or width in studs, the size of individual areas, or the gap/trench dimensions.
- No source gives per-area colors or biomes (aside from "green field").
- No source confirms whether gaps get farther apart deeper in the map. The extra Cosmic and Secret sub-areas suggest more gaps, not necessarily wider spacing.
- Exact base arrangement (bases in a row facing the track? on both sides?) was not confirmed beyond "bases on one side" and "Safe Zone".

## Q3. Tsunami waves (interval, warning, types/speeds, overlap, start/end, visuals, what kills/protects)

### Takeaway
Waves spawn at the far end of the track and travel toward the bases. Touching one is instant death. Natural wave types are named by speed: Super Slow 85, Slow 120, Medium 140, Fast 180, Lightning 220. A purchasable Beast wave runs at 280. Some wiki pages list Wacky/Snakey at 70, and event waves (Radioactive at 85, Electric through admin commands) also exist. To survive, outrun the wave (needs Speed above the wave's speed) or drop into a trench/gap between areas or a VIP wall alcove. A top-screen countdown and siren warn of each wave. Waves come at short intervals and can overlap. The sources disagree on exact timing.

Some entries are "TBA". New waves arrive on "Wave Wednesday". — [Fandom: Waves](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Waves); Lightning 220 also given at [escapetsunamiforbrainnrot fandom: Tsunamis](https://escapetsunamiforbrainnrot.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunamis)
- Beast wave: spawns "when purchased for 8 Wave Tokens from the Wave Machine". ### Inferences
- Wave "speed" and player "Speed" use the same numeric scale (likely Roblox WalkSpeed / studs per second). A wave of speed 140 "matches the average player's movement speed", and players need Speed ≥ the wave's value to outrun it. A recreation can set wave velocity equal to these numbers, in studs/s, against the player's WalkSpeed.
- A wave is a full-width wall of water (except Wacky waves, which are narrow). It moves from the far end toward the bases and passes over the trenches, so a player whose character sits below the wave's lowest point survives. In implementation terms, the kill volume's bottom sits above the trench floor.
- Waves end at or before the safe zone, since bases and the safe zone are safe. The Tsunami Tower is beyond the spawn point or outside the range.
- Several waves can be on the track at once (Fast + Lightning overlapping). Spawn timing is probably a short repeating countdown that picks a random wave type, weighted toward slower waves, with occasional pauses.

### Gaps
- No exact interval in seconds between waves was confirmed. The only figures are MuMuPlayer's 30–60 s loot window and a 3–5 s siren delay, which conflicts with the fan wiki's 15–20 s.
- No confirmed per-wave-type spawn probabilities.
- No wave height or width, nor the exact start and end coordinates.
- Colors are only confirmed for Lightning (purple).

## Q4. Death consequences

### Takeaway
Touching a wave kills you instantly. Any brainrot you were carrying drops on the ground where you died, and you respawn at the safe zone/base. I found no respawn cooldown figure.

### Cited Findings
- "If you touch a Wave, you will die instantly. If you were carrying a Brainrot, it will drop at the exact spot where you died." — [Fandom: Survival Guide](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Survival_Guide)
- If a player is hit by a tsunami, "they respawn at the safe zone". — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots)
- A known glitch: "Resetting your character at the exact millisecond you pick up a Secret-tier Brainrot can let you respawn at base with it already in your hand". This implies the normal respawn point is the base and a normal reset drops the item. — [Fandom: Brainrots](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Brainrots)
- In the separate **Tsunami Arena** mode, waves "fling players upon contact, causing them to drop any Brainrot they are carrying. Dropped Brainrots fall to the ground, allowing others to claim them." Survival there requires a safe zone or a Wave Shield. — [Fandom: Tsunami Arena](https://escape-tsunami-for-brainrots.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunami_Arena)

### Inferences
- Dropped brainrots presumably stay on the ground for some time and can be picked up again by you or by others. That makes a dropped Celestial both a recovery opportunity and a contested item.
